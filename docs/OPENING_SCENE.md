# BIG_O — Opening Scene

Source: founder, real-time, 2026-09-29 ("add the opening scene for BIG_O"). A TYLER crossover cold
open (the TYLER/HANA thread this repo's own `docs/transcript/04-espionage-the-men-tyler-crossover-
hana.md` already scoped) told from the outside: a documentary crew filming Tyler and Hana without
understanding what they're actually documenting. Pasted verbatim; only the scene-heading style is
normalized to standard screenplay format.

---

**INT. SURVEILLANCE VAN — DAWN**

The air in the van is stale, smelling of coffee and heated electronics. The Producer sits hunched
over the monitors, eyes red-rimmed. The Editor manages the audio gain, fingers hovering over dials.
They are not just filming a story; they are "exhausted, addicted to their own dread."

**THE SUBJECT: TYLER**

Monitor 1 shows Tyler standing outside his apartment in the blue-gray dawn light.

> **EDITOR** *(adjusting headphones)*
> He looks... different today. Not scared. Just off.

> **PRODUCER**
> He looks audited. Like he's waiting for someone to check his balance against a ledger.

> **EDITOR**
> We rolling?

> **PRODUCER**
> Always. The footage sells itself. Not online. Somewhere quieter.

**THE INCITING INCIDENT: HANA ARRIVES**

Monitor 2 catches Hana approaching. No knock, no text. She appears "like a clause in a contract."

> **EDITOR**
> She's back. Audio is crisp. Listen to them... they're doing it again. Talking in circles.

> **ON SCREEN (HANA)**
> Small is how it spreads.

> **PRODUCER**
> Spreads? What spreads? Why won't they just say the noun?

> **ON SCREEN (HANA)**
> Don't check anything today... The door you're thinking about.

> **EDITOR** *(whispering)*
> What door? There's no door in the shot.

> **PRODUCER**
> It's not a literal door. It's the conceptual one. The lovers' puzzle. Look at his face — he
> knows exactly what she means.

**THE INTERRUPTION: THE CONTRACTOR**

A sudden knock on the van window. The crew freezes. Outside stands the "nice" Contractor, smiling.

> **CAMERAMAN** *(off-screen, panicked)*
> Are we burned? Someone's at the glass.

> **PRODUCER**
> Cut the feed. Roll down the window.

The Contractor leans in. He doesn't look like security; he looks like he "respawns when attention
turns to risk."

> **CONTRACTOR**
> You're getting followed. No. You're getting priced.

> **PRODUCER**
> We're just a documentary crew. It's just a story.

> **CONTRACTOR**
> Nothing is "just" anymore.

He leaves a card on the dashboard. The Editor flips it over. The text is stark: **DO NOT CHECK**.

> **EDITOR** *(voice shaking)*
> How did he know the code phrase? That's... that's what Hana just said to Tyler.

> **PRODUCER**
> Stop. Don't look at it. Staring straight is safer than turning.

**THE FOLLOW: DETROIT STREETS**

Later. Tyler and Hana act as couriers. The crew follows at a distance, "filming like vultures with
ethics."

> **EDITOR**
> Check the street level. Is it me, or is the city moving out of their way?

> **PRODUCER**
> Focus on the package. The black case.

> **EDITOR**
> He's holding it weird. First like a joke, now like a live animal.

> **PRODUCER**
> He wants to open it. I can feel it from here. He wants to prove the universe can't boss him
> around.

> **EDITOR**
> If he opens it, do we cut?

> **PRODUCER**
> If he opens it, we pray the lens protects us.

**THE DELIVERY: THE ARCHIVIST**

Tyler stops at a basement door. The crew zooms in from across the alley.

> **EDITOR**
> I've got text on the door. Stenciled. ARCHIVIST.

> **PRODUCER**
> That's new. That wasn't in the background research.

The door opens. A gloved hand takes the case. A voice acknowledges the debt.

> **EDITOR**
> Did you get a face? I need a face.

> **PRODUCER**
> No face. Just the hand. And that voice... calm as paperwork.

> **EDITOR**
> He didn't check it. Tyler actually did what she asked.

> **PRODUCER**
> And yet, the alley feels colder.

**POST-PRODUCTION: THE CORRECTION**

Nightfall. Inside the van, the crew reviews the day's dailies. The atmosphere shifts from
observation to participation.

> **EDITOR**
> Okay, playback on the alley scene. Timecode 14:00.

The footage plays. Normal. Then, a jump cut that shouldn't exist.

> **EDITOR**
> Whoa. Glitch. Rewind.

They watch it again. A single frame inserts itself. Tyler looking directly into the lens. He
whispers.

> **AUDIO (TYLER'S VOICE, WRONG TONE)**
> Don't check.

> **EDITOR** *(jumping back)*
> I didn't record that. He never looked at us! He never broke the fourth wall!

> **PRODUCER**
> It's a clean insertion. That's not a glitch. That's... editing.

The Producer's phone buzzes. A notification with no icon: **DEBT COLLECTION: SCHEDULED**.

> **PRODUCER**
> Are we... part of it now?

> **EDITOR**
> Look at the screen. The end of the clip. It's changing.

On the monitor, the footage rearranges itself. It shows the ARCHIVIST door again. But this time,
it's not opening for Tyler.

> **PRODUCER**
> It's opening from our side.

> **EDITOR**
> That hasn't happened yet.

> **PRODUCER**
> It's about to.
