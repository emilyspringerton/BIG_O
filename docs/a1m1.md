# Act I, Mission 1 — Technical Brief

*Source: Gemini chat, 2026-09-18 (raw copy in `docs/source/a1m1-raw.md`). Reformatted for readability only.*

Here is the official Act I, Mission 1 Technical Brief. It arrives as an encrypted text file on your off-the-books basement
terminal screen. The tone is classic corporate passive-aggression: your supervisor, **Dr. Aris Thorne**, is actively using
bureaucratic gaslighting to cover up a brewing disaster while forcing you to break into another division's server rack.

## 📥 INCOMING TRANSMISSION: SECURE_NODE_09

| | |
|---|---|
| **From** | Dr. Aris Thorne, Director of Phenotypic Compliance |
| **To** | Lead Geneticist (Basement Sector-7) |
| **Subject** | DISCIPLINARY REPRIMAND / INTERNAL CORRECTION ASSIGNMENT |
| **Classification** | RESTRICTED COMMUNICADO (Delete After Transcription) |

```
Geneticist,

I have just reviewed your latest alignment logs for Clone Lineage V-04. Frankly, I am profoundly disappointed. Your team
continues to report "unexplained cellular apoptosis" during the 48-hour incubation phase.

Let me be entirely clear: there is no "anomaly" in the baseline pathogen data. There is no cosmic signal. Your data is
sloppy. You are suffering from lab fatigue, and your inability to properly bypass the Nonsense-Mediated Decay (NMD)
restriction flags is a direct reflection of your poor primer configuration, not a flaw in the corporate master template.

However, because your operational sector is off-the-books and the Board cannot afford a complete production halt this
quarter, I am giving you one chance to fix your "errors."

The data you need to stabilize the embryos is locked in the Upper Core. You are to infiltrate Sector-2 tonight.
```

## 📋 MISSION OBJECTIVES

### 🟩 Phase 1: Infiltration & Decorum Maintenance

- **Time-Window:** 23:00 to 03:00 (Standard Night Shift).
- **Asset Requirement:** Acquire a Level-2 Lab Tech Smock from the laundry chute primitives in the maintenance hallway.
- **Tactical Note:** The Sector-2 floor is highly vigilant. If a Senior Peer cards you, do not engage in theoretical biology
  discussions. Use the standard social script matrix: reference the "delightful evening weather" and complain about the
  "quarterly compliance metrics." Do not break decorum.

### 🟩 Phase 2: The Physical Data Swap

- **Target Location:** Air-Gapped Terminal Core, Room 404 (Somatic Sequencing Lab).
- **The Asset:** Locate the physical server stack labeled `PROJECT_METHYLATION_REFS`.
- **The Action:** You cannot download this data over the intranet. You must physically stand behind the automated terminal
  station, wait for the shift supervisor to log in, and use Shoulder-Surfing camera parameters to capture their 8-digit
  Master PIN. Once they leave for their break, slide your physical flash-drive primitive into the terminal chassis.
- **The Data Target:** Pull the file named `FA_REPRESSOR_LOOPS_WGS.fa`. This contains the full-genome reference database of
  synthetic suppression sequences that the shadow labs used to keep the virus's early-termination codons from triggering
  cellular NMD shutdown.

### 🟩 Phase 3: Extraction & Disposal

- Once the data transfer hits 100%, swap the drive.
- If security suspicion ticks up to 2 Stars, grab a janitorial mop primitive on your way out and exit via the service elevator.
- Bring the file back to Sector-7, upload it into your basement centrifuge terminal, and use it to patch the clone embryos.

```
Do not fail this deployment. If internal compliance flags your sector for unauthorized data access, I will personally file
the paperwork to categorize your entire lab team as "Vagrant Hysterics" and hand you over to the Ministry of Public Harmony
for social cancellation.

Clean up your math. The world is perfectly safe.

-- Dr. Thorne
```

**Design notes (added, not part of the source):** the mission maps directly onto B1's rules — costume × zone access
(Level-2 smock for the lab zone), decorum band under a vigilant NPC's "carding" check (`noticed`), the physical-token
gate (vault/terminal access), and the escape path via janitor costume. "Stars" here is the older GTA-style wording;
the implemented model is the Decorum bands (Suspicion → Hysteric → Cancelled). A "Thorne" appears in the TYLER repo (`TYLER/lore/eastwind_archive.md`); whether it is the same Dr. Aris Thorne is unverified — check before treating him as TYLER canon.
